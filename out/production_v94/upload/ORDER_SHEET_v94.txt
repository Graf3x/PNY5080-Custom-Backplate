PG147 COVER - ORDER SHEET v94  (SendCutSend)

MATERIAL  5052-H32 aluminium, 0.080 in (2.0 mm), all five parts.
FINISH    powder coat RAL 9003 signal white, matte or gloss - the
          same on all five. Every part is now over the 1 in powder
          minimum: skins 27.35 mm (1.077 in) wide in the flat.
QUANTITY  1 of each: backplate_bit_plane_v94.dxf, backplate_interference_v94.dxf, carcass_v94.dxf, skin_far_v94.dxf, skin_show_v94.dxf

LAYERS    CUT = laser.  BEND_UP_45 / BEND_DOWN_45 / BEND_DOWN_90 =
          dashed fold lines, direction + angle in the name. UP =
          toward the reader of the file. TAP = drill + tap, not laser.

BACKPLATES  2, interchangeable. 11 x dia 2.90 clearance for the
          cover screws, on CUT. No slots, no taps. The owner
          countersinks these from the BACK by hand after coating
          (90 deg, M2.5 x 0.45 flat head) - the vendor countersinks
          0.125 in and thicker only.

CARCASS   one piece, 10 folds, all 90 deg, all BEND_DOWN_90: nine
          walls and the end cap, all away from the reader. NO feet.
          16 roof vents on CUT. It stands on the skins' feet and is
          bonded to the skins with 3M VHB 4991, 2.30 mm, grey.

SKIN SHOW  199.63 x 27.35 mm flat. 4 x 45 deg folds (two each way) +
          3 foot folds at 90 deg on BEND_DOWN_90, INWARD. 5 tapped
          M2.5 x 0.45 on layer TAP. 6 relief slots 2.0 x 3.47 on the
          bottom edge, one at each foot end - required.
SKIN FAR   199.63 x 27.35 mm flat. 4 x 45 deg folds (two each way) +
          4 foot folds at 90 deg on BEND_DOWN_90, INWARD. 6 tapped
          M2.5 x 0.45 on layer TAP. 8 relief slots 2.0 x 3.47 on the
          bottom edge, one at each foot end - required.

ASSEMBLY  1. Countersink the plate's 11 holes from the back.
          2. Stand each skin on the plate, feet inward, and drive
             M2.5 x 0.45 x 4 flat heads from the back into the feet.
          3. Apply 3M VHB 4991, 2.30 mm, grey to the skins' inner faces,
             drop the carcass in between them onto the feet, press.
          Stack: plate 2.00 + skin foot 2.00 (+coats) = carcass wall
          bottom at 4.45; roof top at 16.95; cable clear height 10.65.
HARDWARE  11 x M2.5 x 0.45 x 4 mm flat head (90 deg), stainless or black.
